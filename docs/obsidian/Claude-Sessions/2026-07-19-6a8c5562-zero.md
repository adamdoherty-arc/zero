written under the old derivation.

RSN-A1's exact bug is documented in `council_service.py:267-271` as fixed for
the voice/chat path. The scheduler caller was never updated.

produced more value than the hunters did.** The crown finding (RET-03) and the
severity *downgrade* (RET-01) both came from checking a hunter's claim against
live data rather than from the claim itself.
