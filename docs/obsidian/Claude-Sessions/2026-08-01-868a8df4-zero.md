## Key Lesson

anyone actually reads.** `_run_with_audit` was always correct; it just never saw a failure.

module import?"** -- `pkgutil.walk_packages` + `importlib` over the app package, 457 modules,
about 20 seconds. Only 2 fail now, both deliberate tombstones.
